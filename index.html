How to Enjoy, Pani Puri Style

3 simple steps to unlock the perfect burst of modern street flavor.

01
👊

Tap

Crack a small hole at the top of the crispy, hollow puri using your thumb or a spoon.

02
🥄

Fill

Add a spoonful of cooked potato-chickpea filling and drizzle some sweet dates paste or cheese dip.

03
😋

Slurp

Pour the chilled mint water concentrate or fiery chilli water inside, and enjoy in a single bite!
