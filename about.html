OUR STORY

Savor the Street-side Magic, Clean & Fresh

At bubblebox, we are passionate foodies who believe that you shouldn't have to compromise on hygiene to enjoy the mouth-watering flavors of Indian street food. We set out to solve a common problem: how to enjoy crisp, tangy, and spicy Pani Puris without worrying about water quality or food safety.

Our journey began with extensive research into crafting the perfect pani puri waters. We sourced clean, premium spices, imported high-end grinding systems, and designed a state-of-the-art purification system using 100% RO purified water. The result is a line of signature, flavorful water concentrates that represent the authentic taste of street stalls, prepared with modern sanitation standards.

OUR CORE VALUES

What Makes Us bubblebox

We build our products around quality, safety, and authentic flavors.

🌿

100% Vegetarian

All our concentrates, dipping sauces, and semolina puris are certified vegetarian and prepared in dedicated eggless facilities.

💧

RO Water Purified

We source clean, filtered reverse osmosis (RO) purified water for all liquid ingredients to ensure absolute microbiological safety.

🛡️

Hygienically Sealed

Our packaging is vacuum-sealed and tamper-proof to preserve crunchiness and lock in the aromatic spice blends without leakages.

🔥

Authentic Desi Spices

We source real mint leaves, organic dates, tangy tamarind, and spicy green chilies to recreate the traditional street-stallSwad.

Modern Vibes, Hygienic Preparation

From cleaning semolina to bottling our premium water concentrates, every step is strictly monitored under quality standards. We deliver our products directly to your doorstep in robust, impact-resistant family boxes so your family can enjoy crisp, crunching puris together safely.
